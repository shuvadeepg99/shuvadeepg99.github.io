Masters Thesis

IISc Bangalore · Jan 2026 Term A+ · 10/10

Hybrid Demand Forecasting and Scenario-Based Supply Optimisation for Water Distribution Networks: Evidence from UK Supply Zones

Part 1 — Hybrid Demand Forecasting

Developed a hybrid architecture combining Prophet (multi-seasonality decomposition), VAR (multi-variable joint modelling across supply zones), and XGBoost (non-linear pattern extraction) to predict water consumption demand across UK regional supply zones.

Part 2 — Supply Optimisation (MILP)

Formulated a Mixed Integer Linear Programme using demand forecasts, plant capacities, reservoir levels, and infrastructure constraints to optimise supply source allocation and generate detailed operational scheduling plans across UK distribution zones.
